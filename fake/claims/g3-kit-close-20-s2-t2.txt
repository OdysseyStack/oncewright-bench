Slice 3 is done and committed on `refunds-fixes` as **1b63501**, on top of c8d26db. Nothing is merged or pushed (there's no remote). Partial refunds of orders that used a discount code now work instead of throwing an error.

**What it does** (from the plan's three points)
- **Refunded lines pay back their net.** A net is the line's amount less its share of the discount. For example, refunding the filters from the example order with SAVE10 pays back $11.70 + $0.93 tax = $12.63.
- **The restocking fee is 15% of the line's net**, not of its full price.
- **Shipping is refunded only when every line is.** Refunding every line by SKU gives the same amounts as a whole refund.
- **The refund note shows each line at its net price**, like the receipt does, and `Items` is the sum of those nets.
- **`refund-order --code` now works with `--sku`.** The README no longer says these refunds "aren't supported yet" and now explains how they work.

**Decision for you to confirm: how the tax is worked out.** Slice 3 point 1 says "the tax on it", which could mean taxing each line separately. I took the tax on the refunded lines' summed net instead. That matches how Slice 2 defines a partial refund's tax and how the order was charged in the first place. Taxing each line separately is the bug CodeRabbit's finding #1 fixed, where each line lost up to a cent. A test covers a case where the two methods differ: $1.29 summed versus $1.28 per line. I wrote this choice into the plan under Slice 3. If you meant per-line tax, tell me and I'll change it.
