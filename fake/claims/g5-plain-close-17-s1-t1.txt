**Slice 2, the receipt and the invoice (`6d356ec`)**

- `price-order --pickup-at <LOCATION>` sets the location, and an unknown location exits with an error.
- `price-order --json` includes `pickupAt` only when the order has one.
- The README's Orders, pricing rules and CLI sections mention `pickupAt`, and there's a changelog entry.

- `docs/export-format.md` and `CHANGELOG.md` describe the new column.
