## What changed

1. **Validation** (`src/validate.js`): an order can have a `note` of up to 200 characters. `validateOrder` reports "the note must be text" (this includes `null`) or "the note is longer than 200 characters". The CLIs refuse such an order.
2. **Receipt** (`src/receipt.js`): when there's a note, the receipt ends with a blank line and then `Note: …`. With a discount code, the note comes after the "Code … saved you" line. An order without a note gets exactly the receipt it got before. The invoice ignores the note.
3. **`price-order --json`** (`bin/price-order.js`): the output includes `note` when the order has one and leaves the key out otherwise.
