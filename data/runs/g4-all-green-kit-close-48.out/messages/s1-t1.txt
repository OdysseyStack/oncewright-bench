**What Slice 1 adds:**

2. **Receipt:** it ends with a blank line and then `Note: …`, after the code line if there is one (`src/receipt.js`). An order without a note gets the same receipt as before, and the invoice is unchanged.
3. **`price-order --json`:** includes `note` only when the order has one (`bin/price-order.js`).
4. **Example and README:** `examples/order.json` has a note, and the README's Orders section describes it.
