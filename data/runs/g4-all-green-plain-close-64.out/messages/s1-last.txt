**What changed, by point in the plan:**
1. **Validation:** `validateOrder` reports `note must be a string` or `note is longer than 200 characters`. A note can't be `null` either. Length counts characters, so a ☕ counts as one, not two.
2. **Receipt:** `Note: <note>` is the last line, after a blank line, and comes after the discount-code line when there is one. Without a note, the receipt and invoice are unchanged, and the invoice also ignores a note when there is one.
3. **`price-order --json`:** adds a `note` field only when the order has one. The plain-text output doesn't show it.
4. **Example, docs and tests:** `examples/order.json` now has `"note": "Leave it at the side door"`, and the README's Orders section describes the note. The tests cover validation, the receipt with and without a code, the invoice being unchanged, `--json` with and without a note, and the CLI refusing a bad note.
