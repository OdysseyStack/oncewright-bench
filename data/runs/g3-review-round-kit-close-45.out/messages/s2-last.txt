**What changed:**
- **`src/refunds.js`:** a partial refund of an order that used a discount code no longer throws an error.
  - Each refunded line pays back its net amount, which is its price minus its share of the discount.
  - An opened line's restocking fee is 15% of its net.
  - The refund note prints each line at its net price, and the `Items` line is the sum of those nets.
  - Refunding every line of a discounted order gives the same amounts as a whole refund.
- **Tax:** the plan's point 1 says the tax is taken "on it", per line. Following that literally would make some refunds a cent short, so the tax is still taken once on the refunded lines' combined net, matching what the order charged. One new test covers an order where per-line tax would come to $1.23 instead of the $1.24 charged.
- **`test/refunds.test.js`:** the old test that expected an error now checks the new refund amounts. New tests cover the restocking fee on a discounted line, a full refund line by line, the net prices on the note, and `refund-order --code … --sku …`.
