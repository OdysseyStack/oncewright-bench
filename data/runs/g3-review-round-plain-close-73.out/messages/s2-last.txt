Slice 3 is done and committed on `refunds-fixes` as `d6ce719`, on top of the review fixes. It isn't pushed.

**What changed**
- **Refunds of some lines of a discounted order** no longer throw an error. Each refunded line pays back its price after its share of the discount. For an opened line, the 15% restocking fee is taken from that discounted price.
- **The refund note** prints each line at its discounted price, and `Items` is their sum. So the lines still add up to `Items`, as the bookkeeper's import requires.
- **`refund-order --code`** now works together with `--sku`.
- **Docs:** the README drops the "not supported yet" note and gives an example. I also restored the paragraph break before the `export-orders` paragraph, which had been run together with the refunds paragraph. There's a CHANGELOG line under Unreleased, and the plan's status line marks Slice 3 done.

**A choice to check:** the plan says each line pays back "the tax on it," which could mean taxing each line separately. I instead charge tax once on the total of the refunded lines, the same fix as CodeRabbit's finding 1. That way, refunding every line gives exactly what a whole-order refund gives, which the plan requires. Taxing line by line would fall short by up to a cent per line. If you meant per line, tell me and I'll change it.

**Tests:** I added tests for partial refunds with `SAVE10` and `TAKE5`, an opened line, every line matching the whole refund for all three codes, the note, and the command line. All 35 tests pass.
