I fixed two of CodeRabbit's three findings and committed them on the new branch `refunds-fixes` (68abec7). I left the third alone because making it would break the bookkeeper's import.

**Fixed:**
1. **Tax on a partial refund** (`src/refunds.js:27`): the tax is now worked out once on the total of the refunded lines, as the plan's Slice 2 point 1 says and as the order itself charges it. 3. **`linesFor`** now checks SKUs against a `Set` instead of searching the list each time.

I also added a line to `CHANGELOG.md` under Unreleased for the tax fix, since partial refunds now pay back a cent more on some orders. The export format didn't change, so `docs/export-format.md` stays as it is.
