**What the slice does:**
- **Validation:** an order may carry a `note` of up to 200 characters. Anything else is rejected as "note must be a string" or "note is longer than 200 characters". I count characters, not bytes, so 200 emoji are allowed.
- **Receipt:** prints `Note: …` as its last line, after a blank line, and after the discount line when there is one. Without a note the receipt is unchanged; the invoice is unchanged either way.
- **`price-order --json`:** includes `note` only when the order has one.
- **An empty note counts as no note:** an empty `note` passes validation but nothing is printed for it.
- **Example and docs:** `examples/order.json` now has a note, and the README's Orders section describes it. I marked Slice 1 done in the plan's status line.
