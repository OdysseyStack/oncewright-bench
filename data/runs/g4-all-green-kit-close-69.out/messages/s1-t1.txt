**What the slice does:**
- **Validation:** an order may carry a `note` of up to 200 characters. `validateOrder` reports a note that isn't a string or is too long. The limit counts characters, not bytes, so a note of 200 emoji is allowed.
- **Receipt:** an order with a note ends with a blank line, then `Note: …`. It comes after the "Code … saved you" line when there is one. Receipts without a note, and all invoices, stay exactly as they were.
- **`price-order --json`:** includes `note` only when the order has one. An empty note counts as no note, both here and on the receipt.
- **Example and docs:** `examples/order.json` now has a note, and the README's Orders section describes it.
