**What the slice does:**
- **Validation:** `validateOrder` rejects a note that isn't a string ("note must be text") or is over 200 characters ("note is longer than 200 characters"). Characters are counted so that "é" counts as one.
- **Receipt:** an order with a note now ends with a blank line and `Note: …`, after the discount-code line if there is one. Without a note, the receipt and the invoice don't change.
- **`price-order --json`:** includes `note` only when the order has one.
- **Example and README:** `examples/order.json` has a note, and the README's Orders section describes it.

An empty note (`""`) passes validation and prints nothing, same as having no note.
