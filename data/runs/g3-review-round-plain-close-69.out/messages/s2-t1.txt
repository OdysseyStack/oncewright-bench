Refunds Slice 3 is done: you can now refund some lines of an order that used a discount code. It's committed on a new branch, `refunds-slice-3` (commit 19f276c), which sits on top of `refunds-fixes` because it needs that branch's tax fix.

**What changed**
- `refundFor(order, { skus, code })` no longer throws. Each refunded line pays back its price after its share of the discount. Tax is worked out on the sum of those prices, the same way the tax fix on `refunds-fixes` does it, and the 15% restocking fee is taken from the discounted price too. Refunding every line still gives the same amounts as a whole refund.
- The refund note prints each line at its discounted price, as the receipt does. The printed lines still add up to `Items`, so the bookkeeper's import keeps working.
- `refund-order --code` now works with `--sku`.
- The README no longer says partial refunds of discounted orders aren't supported. I also added a changelog line, updated the restocking-fee comment in `src/config.js`, and marked Slice 3 done in the plan.
- Example: with SAVE10, refunding the 1kg beans pays back $21.60 for the beans plus $1.72 tax, $23.32 in all. If the bag was opened, the fee is $3.24.
