What the slice does:
- A partial refund of an order with a discount code no longer throws an error. Each refunded line pays back its amount minus its share of the discount.
- Tax is 8% of those discounted line amounts added together.
- The 15% restocking fee is taken from the discounted amount.
- The refund note shows each line at its discounted price, and the restocking fee stays on its own line.
- Refunding every line of a discounted order gives the same amounts as before.
- `--code` now works with `--sku`.
