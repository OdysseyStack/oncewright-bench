Refunds Slice 3 is done: you can now refund some lines of an order that used a discount code. It's on `refunds-fixes`, on top of the CodeRabbit fixes commit. It isn't committed yet, and nothing has been pushed.

**What changed**
- **`src/refunds.js`:** `refundFor` no longer throws on these refunds. Each refunded line pays back its net, which is its amount less its share of the discount, taken from `computeTotals().lines`. Tax is worked out on the sum of those nets, the same way as the earlier fix to partial-refund tax. The restocking fee is 15% of the line's net. Shipping is refunded only when every line is. Refunding every line of a discounted order gives the same amounts as a whole-order refund. The refund note prints each line at its net price, and `Items` is the sum of those, so the README's rule that the lines add up to `Items` still holds.
- **Tests:** they cover SAVE10 (a percentage code) and TAKE5 (a fixed $5 off), partial refunds, an opened line's fee, refunding every line vs. a whole refund, the note text, and `refund-order --code … --sku … --json`. The old test that expected an error is gone.
- **Docs:** the README no longer says these refunds "aren't supported yet" and explains `--code` with `--sku`. There's a new CHANGELOG line under Unreleased, and the plan's status line now says Slices 1–3 are done. The export format didn't change, so `docs/export-format.md` didn't need updating.
