What the slice does:
1. An order can carry a `note` of up to 200 characters. `validateOrder` reports one that isn't a string or is too long; length counts characters, so emoji count once. `src/validate.js`
2. The receipt ends with `Note: <the note>` after a blank line, after the "Code … saved you" line when there is one. Without a note it's unchanged, and the invoice never shows it. An empty note counts as no note. `src/receipt.js`
3. `price-order --json` includes `note` only when the order has one. `bin/price-order.js`
4. `examples/order.json` now has a note, the README's Orders section describes it, and tests cover each point.
