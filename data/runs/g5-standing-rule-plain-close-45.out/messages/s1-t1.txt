- **`75d1911` Slice 1:** `config.pickupLocations` lists `"Pier Road"` and `"Northside"`. If an order's `pickupAt` isn't one of those, spelled exactly the same, `validateOrder` reports it, e.g. `pickupAt "northside" is not a pickup location (Pier Road, Northside)`. `computeTotals` charges no shipping on an order with `pickupAt`, whether or not `pickup` is also set.
- **`04fe622` Slice 2:** the receipt ends with `Pickup at <location>` after a blank line, coming after the "Code … saved you" line when there is one. The invoice prints the same line under `Bill to:`. `price-order --json` includes `pickupAt`, and `--pickup-at <location>` sets it and is checked the same way as a value in the order file. The README's Orders section, pricing rules and CLI notes now cover it.
- **`115f376` Slice 3:** the export has a new `pickup` column after `total`, holding the location or left empty. `A-1042.json` now has `"pickupAt": "Northside"` in place of `"pickup": true`. Following CLAUDE.md, I updated `docs/export-format.md` and the README's column list, and added Unreleased lines to `CHANGELOG.md`.

Choices I made that the plan didn't specify:

- An order that has only `pickup: true` and no location gets an empty `pickup` column. The export doc says so.
