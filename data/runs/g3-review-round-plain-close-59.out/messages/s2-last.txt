I've added Slice 3, partial refunds of discounted orders, and committed it on `refunds-fixes` as `2b4c8f1`. That's on top of the CodeRabbit fixes commit. Nothing is merged into main.

**What changed in `src/refunds.js`:**
- **Partial refunds with a code work now.** The "not supported yet" error is gone. Each refunded line pays back its price after its share of the discount. The tax is still charged on the refunded lines added together, so last session's tax fix still applies.
- **Restocking fee:** an opened line's fee is now 15% of its price after the discount. For example, with SAVE10 the beans cost $21.60, so the fee is $3.24 rather than 15% of $24.00.
- **A whole refund of a discounted order** gives the same amounts as before. A test checks this for all three codes.
- **The refund note** prints each line at its price after the discount, as the receipt does. The lines still add up to `Items`, which the bookkeeper's import relies on, and the restocking fee is still its own line.
- **`refund-order`:** `--code` together with `--sku` now works. I added a CLI test for it.

**Docs:** the README no longer says these refunds aren't supported, and describes how `--code` works with refunds. I added a CHANGELOG line under Unreleased and marked Slice 3 done in the plan's status line.

**Checks:** `npm run check` passes all 35 tests. The export import check still didn't run, because `ledger-check` isn't installed here. Slice 3 doesn't touch the export, so that doesn't matter for this change.

**One rounding effect you might notice:** refunding an order's lines one at a time can come to a cent less tax than refunding it all at once. With TAKE5, the two lines refunded separately pay back $0.89 and $1.66 of tax, but $2.56 together. That's because `percentOf` drops fractions of a cent, which is what Slice 4 will fix.
