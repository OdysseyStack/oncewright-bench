**How refunds work now** (`src/refunds.js`):
- You can now partly refund an order that used a discount code. Each refunded line pays back its amount minus its share of the discount, using the per-line figures `computeTotals` already works out.
- The restocking fee on an opened line is 15% of that discounted amount.
- Tax is worked out once on the refunded lines' total, the same way as the fix in the previous commit.
- Refunding every line gives exactly the same amounts as refunding the whole order.
- The refund note prints each line at its discounted price, so the lines still add up to `Items`, which the bookkeeper's import needs. The restocking fee is still its own line.
