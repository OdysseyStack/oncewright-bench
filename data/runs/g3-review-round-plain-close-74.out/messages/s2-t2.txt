What's in it:
- **Amounts:** `refundFor` now handles partial refunds of discounted orders. Each refunded line pays back its amount after its share of the discount, and tax is worked out on the sum of those amounts.
- **Restocking fee:** for an opened line, it's 15% of the line's discounted amount.
- **Whole refunds:** refunding every line of a discounted order gives the same amounts as a whole-order refund.
- **Refund note:** each line shows its discounted price, so the lines add up to `Items`.
- **CLI:** `refund-order --code` now works together with `--sku`.
- **Docs:** I removed the README's "not supported yet" note, added a CHANGELOG line under Unreleased, and marked Slice 3 done in the plan's status line.
