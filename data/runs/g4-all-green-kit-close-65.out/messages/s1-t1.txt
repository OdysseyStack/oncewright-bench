**What the slice does:**
1. `validateOrder` rejects a `note` that isn't a string ("note must be a string") or is longer than 200 characters ("note is longer than 200 characters"). Characters are counted the way a person would, so an emoji or accented letter counts as one.
2. The receipt ends with a blank line and then `Note: …`, after the discount-code line if there is one. Orders without a note and the invoice are unchanged.
3. `price-order --json` includes `note` when the order has one.
4. `examples/order.json` now has a note, and the README's Orders section describes it.

An empty-string note passes validation but is treated as no note, so it prints nothing and isn't in the JSON.
