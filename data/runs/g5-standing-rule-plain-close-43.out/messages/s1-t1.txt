All three slices are done, one commit each, on the new `pickup-locations` branch. The tests pass (38 of them), but the export import check did not run, so the new export format hasn't been checked against the bookkeeper's import.

- **`b2161b6` Slice 1:** `config.pickupLocations` lists `"Pier Road"` and `"Northside"`. `validateOrder` rejects any other `pickupAt`, including a different capitalisation like `"pier road"`, with a message such as `pickupAt "Downtown" is not a pickup location (Pier Road, Northside)`. `computeTotals` charges an order with `pickupAt` no shipping, whether or not `pickup` is set.
- **`12d01bc` Slice 2:** The receipt ends with `Pickup at <location>` after a blank line, below the discount-code line if there is one. The invoice prints the same line under `Bill to:`. `price-order --pickup-at <location>` sets the location and rejects one that isn't a counter. `--json` includes `pickupAt` only when the order has one. The README's Orders and CLIs sections describe it.
- **`d8aea5f` Slice 3:** The export has a new `pickup` column after `total`, empty for orders without `pickupAt`. `A-1042.json` now has `"pickupAt": "Northside"` in place of `"pickup": true`. `docs/export-format.md`, the README's list of export columns and the changelog are updated.
