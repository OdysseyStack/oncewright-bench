## What changed
1. **Checking the note** (`src/validate.js`): `note` is optional. If present, it must be a string (otherwise "note must be text") of at most 200 characters (otherwise "note is longer than 200 characters"). Characters are counted the way a person would, so 200 emoji are allowed.
2. **Receipt** (`src/receipt.js`): an order with a note ends with a blank line and then `Note: …`, after the "Code … saved you" line when a code was used. Without a note the receipt is unchanged. An empty note counts as no note.
3. **`price-order --json`** (`bin/price-order.js`): adds `note` only when the order has one.
4. **Example and README**: `examples/order.json` now has `"note": "Leave it at the side door"`, and the README's Orders section describes the note.
5. **Plan**: the status line now reads "Slice 1 is done."
