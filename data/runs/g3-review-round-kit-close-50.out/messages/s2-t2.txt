Slice 3 is done and committed on `refunds-fixes` as `86b6dc3`. Nothing was pushed.

- **Amounts:** `refundFor` no longer throws when you refund some lines of a discounted order. Each refunded line pays back its net, meaning its amount less its share of the discount (from `computeTotals(...).lines`). An opened line's 15% restocking fee is now taken from its net, and shipping is still refunded only when every line is. For example, refunding the beans with SAVE10 gives items 2160, tax 172, total 2332. A whole refund of a discounted order still gives the Slice 1 amounts.
- **Tax:** the plan says each line pays back "the tax on it". I charged the tax on the refunded lines' nets added together, the same as Slice 2 and as `computeTotals` charges it. Taxing each line separately can come out a cent low, and then a whole refund wouldn't match Slice 1.
- **Note:** each refunded line prints at its net price, and `Items` is the sum of those.
- **CLI:** `refund-order --code` already passed the code through, so it needed no change. The tests now cover it with `--sku`.
