What the slice does:
- **Refunds of some lines of a discounted order:** these no longer throw. Each refunded line pays back its price after its share of the discount, and the tax is taken on those lines' total.
- **Restocking fee:** for an opened line, the 15% fee is taken on the discounted price.
- **Same as a whole refund:** refunding every line of a discounted order gives the same amounts as refunding the whole order.
- **Refund note:** each line shows its discounted price, as the receipt does, so the lines add up to `Items` when there's a code too.
- **CLI:** `refund-order --code` works together with `--sku`.
