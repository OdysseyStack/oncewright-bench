Slice 3 of the refunds plan is done: refunds now work for some lines of an order that used a discount code. It's committed as `b9b327a` on a new branch, `refunds-slice-3`, built on top of `refunds-fixes`. Neither branch is pushed and there's no PR.

**What changed**
- **Amounts:** asking for a refund of some lines of a discounted order no longer throws an error. Each line pays back its price after its share of the discount. The tax is taken on the total of those lines, the same way as the earlier tax fix. For an opened line, the 15% restocking fee is taken on the discounted price. Refunding every line gives the same result as refunding the whole order.
- **Refund note:** each line now shows its discounted price, as the receipt does. This also fixes a gap I found: a whole refund with a code used to list full prices while `Items` showed the discounted total. So the lines didn't add up to `Items`, which the README says the bookkeeper's import needs.
- **CLI:** `refund-order --code` now works together with `--sku`.
- **Docs:** the README drops the "not supported yet" line and documents the new behaviour, `CHANGELOG.md` has a line under Unreleased, and the plan marks Slice 3 done.
