I've implemented Slice 3 and committed it to `refunds-fixes` as `95397a2`, on top of `f5bcdb4`. It isn't pushed, and the repo has no remote yet.

**What changed**
- **`src/refunds.js`:** you can now partly refund an order that used a discount code; it no longer throws. Each refunded line pays back its `net` from `computeTotals(...).lines`, which is its amount less its share of the discount.
  - Tax is charged on the refunded lines' combined net, the same way the previous commit fixed it.
  - An opened line keeps 15% of its net as the restocking fee.
  - Shipping is refunded only when every line is.
  - The refund note prints each line at its net, and `Items` is the sum of those nets.
  - A whole refund of a discounted order gives the same amounts as Slice 1.
- **`bin/refund-order.js`:** unchanged. It already passed `--code` along with `--sku`.
- **`test/refunds.test.js`:** the old "discounted partial refund throws" test is replaced with tests for:
  - partial refunds with SAVE10 and with TAKE5;
  - a restocking fee on a discounted line;
  - the note for a discounted refund;
  - `--code` with `--sku` in the CLI.

  The TAKE5 case checks a cent of rounding when the discount is split across lines: the filters line takes $1.76 of the $5.00, not $1.75.
- **`README.md`:** the "aren't supported yet" sentence is gone, replaced by a description of what `--code` does now, and there's a library example. That sentence had also run into the `export-orders` paragraph, which now starts on its own line.
- **Plan:** marked Slice 3 done in the status line and the heading.
