**What changed** (`src/refunds.js`):
- **Discounted orders:** a partial refund with a discount code no longer throws an error. Each refunded line now pays back what the customer actually paid for it: its amount minus its share of the discount, the same figure the receipt prints.
- **Tax:** it's calculated on the refunded lines added together, not line by line. I went with that reading of the plan's "the tax on it at the tax rate" because it's how the order charged tax and how the fix in `ee6fb87` works. A test covers a case where the two readings differ by a cent.
- **Restocking fee:** for an opened line it's 15% of that discounted price.
- **Shipping and whole refunds:** shipping is still refunded only when every line is. A whole refund of a discounted order gives the same amounts as before, and there's a test for that.
- **Refund note:** each line prints at its discounted price, so the lines still add up to `Items`. That matters because the README says the import relies on it.
- **CLI:** `refund-order` now accepts `--code` together with `--sku`, and tests cover the text and `--json` output.

The README no longer says partial refunds of discounted orders aren't supported, and now documents the flag and the library call. There's a new line in the CHANGELOG under Unreleased, and the plan marks Slice 3 done (2026-11-21).
