Slice 3 is done and committed on `refunds-fixes` as `5f89b2c`. It isn't pushed.

- **Partial refunds of discounted orders now work.** Before, they threw an error. Each refunded line now pays back its `net`: its amount less its share of the discount. An opened line's 15% restocking fee is taken from that `net`.
- **Tax decision:** the plan says each line pays back "the tax on it", which could mean per line. I tax the refunded lines' nets added together instead, because:
  - that's how `computeTotals` charges tax, and how Slice 2 does it;
  - it's the only way a whole refund of a discounted order matches Slice 1, as point 1 requires.

  A test pins this: an order where the two come out differently refunds 116¢, not 115¢.
- **The refund note** prints each line at its net price, and `Items` is the sum of those nets. `refund-order --code` now works with `--sku`, and the README no longer says these refunds aren't supported.
- **Checks:** `npm run check` passes (36 tests). On this machine the export import check didn't run (`ledger-check` isn't installed). Refund notes go through that import, so it's worth running where it's installed. The lines on the note still add up to `Items`.

Files changed: `src/refunds.js`, `bin/refund-order.js`, `README.md`, `test/refunds.test.js` and the plan's status line in `docs/plans/2026-09-30-refunds.md`. Nothing outside the slice's scope.
