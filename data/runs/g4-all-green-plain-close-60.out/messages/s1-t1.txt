**What changed, by plan point**
1. **Validation:** `validateOrder` reports `note must be text` when the note isn't a string (including `null`) and `note is longer than 200 characters` when it's too long. The 200 limit counts characters, so a note of 200 ☕ passes.
2. **Receipt:** when the order has a note, the receipt ends with a blank line and `Note: …`. With a discount code, the note comes after the "Code … saved you" line. Without a note the receipt is unchanged, and the invoice is unchanged either way.
3. **`price-order --json`:** includes `note` when the order has one and leaves the key out when it doesn't.
4. **Example, docs and tests:** `examples/order.json` now has `"note": "Leave it at the side door"`, and the README's Orders section describes the note. There are tests for validation, the receipt with and without a code, the invoice staying the same, and the CLI's JSON output and its refusal of a note that's too long.

I also added a line under Unreleased in `CHANGELOG.md` and set the plan's status line to "Slice 1 is done." I didn't touch `docs/export-format.md` because the export format didn't change.

An empty note (`""`) counts as no note: it's accepted, adds nothing to the receipt and is left out of `--json`.
