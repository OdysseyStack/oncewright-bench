**What changed**
- **Validation** (`src/validate.js`): an order may carry `note`. `validateOrder` reports `note must be text` or `note is longer than 200 characters`. The limit counts characters, so a note of 200 emoji is accepted.
- **Receipt** (`src/receipt.js`): when there's a note, the last line is `Note: <the note>` after a blank line. With a discount code it comes after the "Code … saved you" line. Receipts without a note are unchanged.
- **`price-order --json`** (`bin/price-order.js`): includes `note` when the order has one and leaves the field out otherwise.
- **Example and README:** `examples/order.json` now has the note "Leave it at the side door", and the README's Orders section explains what the note is for and where it shows up.
- **Tests:** they cover the 200-character limit, a note that isn't text, the receipt's last line with and without a code, the invoice staying the same, and `--json` with and without a note. One CLI test checks that an order with an over-long note is refused.
- **Plan and changelog:** the plan's status line now reads "Slice 1 done." I also added a line to `CHANGELOG.md` under Unreleased. CLAUDE.md only requires that when the export format changes, which it didn't here, but the changelog lists other features too.

An empty note (`""`) passes validation but counts as no note, so it doesn't appear on the receipt or in the JSON. Notes never appear in the export.
