**What changed in `src/refunds.js`**
- A partial refund of a discounted order no longer throws an error. Each refunded line pays back its price minus its share of the discount, using the per-line split `computeTotals` already works out.
- Tax is worked out on the total of those lines together, as before.
- The 15% restocking fee is taken from the discounted price, not the original one.
- Shipping is still refunded only when every line is.
- The refund note prints each line at its discounted price, and those lines add up to `Items`. The restocking fee stays on its own line, as the README's import rule requires.
- A whole refund comes out the same as before. A test refunds every line with each of the three codes and checks that the result matches the whole-order refund.

**Example:** on the A-1040 order with SAVE10, refunding just the filters gives $11.70 for the items, $0.93 tax and $12.63 in total.
