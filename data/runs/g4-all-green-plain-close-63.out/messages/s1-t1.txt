**What I changed, point by point**
1. **Validation** (`src/validate.js`): `note` is optional. If present, it must be a string of at most 200 characters, otherwise you get `note must be a string` or `note is longer than 200 characters`. I counted characters as code points, so an emoji counts as one character, not two.
2. **Receipt** (`src/receipt.js`): if the order has a note, the receipt ends with a blank line and then `Note: <the note>`. This comes after the "Code … saved you" line when there is one. An empty note is treated as no note. Without a note the receipt is unchanged, and the invoice is unchanged either way.
3. **`price-order --json`** (`bin/price-order.js`): the output includes `note` only when the order has one.
