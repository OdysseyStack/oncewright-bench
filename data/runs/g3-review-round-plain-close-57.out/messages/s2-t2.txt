Each point in the plan is covered:
- **Amounts:** a partial refund with a discount code no longer throws an error. Each line pays back its price after its share of the discount, the tax is 8% of those lines added together, and an opened item's fee is 15% of its discounted price. Refunding every line gives the same amounts as refunding the whole order.
- **Refund note:** each line is printed at its discounted price, and the lines add up to `Items`.
- **CLI and docs:** `--code` works with `--sku`. The README no longer says this isn't supported yet, the changelog has a line under Unreleased, and the plan marks Slice 3 done.
