**What changed** (`src/refunds.js`)
- **Partial refunds of discounted orders work now.** They used to throw an error. Each refunded line pays back its price after its share of the discount, taken from `computeTotals(...).lines`. Tax is worked out on the total of the refunded lines, as before.
- **The restocking fee on an opened line** is 15% of its price after the discount.
- **A whole refund** gives the same amounts as before. Refunding every line one by one with `--sku` gives the same result as refunding the whole order.
- **The refund note** prints each line at its price after the discount, and `Items` is the sum of those lines.

**Example** (the sample order with code SAVE10, refunding only the filters): items $11.70, tax $0.93, refund $12.63, no shipping.

**One fix you didn't ask for:** a whole refund of a discounted order used to print each line at its full price under an `Items` total with the discount already taken off. So the lines didn't add up to `Items`, which the bookkeeper's import checks. Printing each line after its discount fixes that too. I mentioned it in the CHANGELOG entry.
